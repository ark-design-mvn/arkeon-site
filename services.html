Services

Schedule of services

Residential design and documentation, from first sketch to approved plans. Eight core services, and four more by arrangement.

Core
S-01Residential concept designConcepts, space planning and layouts developed around how you live and what the site offers.
S-02Council submission drawingsDrawings and documents for building plan approval, prepared to SANS 10400 and municipal requirements.
S-03Alterations and additionsExtensions, renovations, garages, patios and braai rooms that sit naturally with the existing house.
S-04As-built and site measuresMeasuring up on site and drawing existing buildings, including work never recorded on approved plans.
S-053D visualisationModels and rendered views that show clearly what is proposed before anything is built.
S-06Technical drawingsPlans, sections, elevations and construction details a builder can price and build from.
S-07Estate and HOA submissionsDrawings prepared against estate design guidelines for review and approval.
S-08Site planning and assessmentBuilding lines, zoning, coverage and placement: what your property can take.
By arrangement
S-09Heritage submission supportDrawings and supporting documents for heritage approval processes.
S-10Drafting supportCAD and Revit drafting for architects, technologists and other professionals.
S-11Regularisation drawingsDocumenting unapproved building work for submission, subject to municipal requirements.
S-12Preliminary consultationsEarly guidance on site constraints, feasibility and the approvals you are likely to need.

Every service ends in clear drawings like this one. See more in Work.
