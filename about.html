Focus
Residential design and documentation

Measuring existing homes, understanding how they work, and drawing for light and slope.

I work on residential projects from the first measured sketch through to clear, buildable documentation. Site conditions, daylight and the fall of the land shape the design, so homes feel comfortable, efficient and connected to their setting.

BArk + Eon

The ARKEON name

ARKEON draws on the Greek arkhitektōn, the master builder: one who conceives, measures and brings a work into being. "Ark" also recalls the ark, a building specified down to its dimensions in cubits. "Eon" comes from aiōn, an age: a long span of time.

Together: buildings that are planned with care, documented with precision, and made to last.
