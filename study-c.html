Hillside

The brief

A house with wide views over False Bay, on a steep plot, with as little cut and fill as possible.

The site

A plot falling steeply to the south-west, with views across the bay and exposure to the prevailing wind.

The response

A single floor plate sits on two lines of supports and cantilevers over the slope, so the ground below stays largely untouched.

AProcess

From sketch to section

The study moves from a loose sketch to a resolved section and a massing model, testing form, light and the relationship to the outdoors.
