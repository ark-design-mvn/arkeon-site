Addition

The brief

More living space and a better connection to the garden, without changing the character of the existing house from the street.

The site

An existing gabled house with a deep rear garden; the new work sits behind the building line, away from the neighbours' windows.

The response

A low, flat-roofed pavilion is set against the back of the house. It reads as a separate, lighter piece and opens fully to the garden.

AProcess

From sketch to section

The study moves from a loose sketch to a resolved section and a massing model, testing form, light and the relationship to the outdoors.
