Courtyard

The brief

A single-storey family home with open, light-filled living, a calm private courtyard and a simple, efficient footprint.

The site

A suburban plot with a gentle fall, street access to the south and the best sun and shelter on the north side.

The response

An L-shaped plan wraps a north-facing courtyard. The two wings shelter it from the south-easter, and the roofs rise towards the courtyard to bring winter sun deep into the rooms.

AProcess

From sketch to section

The study moves from a loose sketch to a resolved section and a massing model, testing form, light and the relationship to the outdoors.
