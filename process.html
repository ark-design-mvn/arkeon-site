Process

How a project runs

Projects follow the work stages used across the South African architectural profession, so you always know what has been agreed and what comes next.

AWork stages

What you receive at each stage

Stage 1

Inception

We meet, visit the site and agree the brief, budget and timeline. We check zoning, building lines and title deed conditions.

You receive: a written brief and fee proposal.

Stage 2

Concept and viability

Two or three layout options, tested against the site, orientation and wind.

You receive: sketch plans and massing views.

Stage 3

Design development

The chosen option is refined into a full design, with materials agreed.

You receive: plans, sections, elevations and 3D views.

Stage 4

Documentation

Drawings for council and, where needed, estate or heritage approval, plus the technical set for pricing.

You receive: submission and technical drawings.

BGetting started

The first meeting

  1. You send a short enquiry about your property and what you have in mind.
  2. We arrange a call or a site visit to talk through the brief.
  3. You receive a written proposal setting out scope, stages and fees.
Helpful to have ready

Your erf number or address, any existing approved plans, a few photos of the house or site, and a rough budget.

CApprovals

Which approvals your project may need

01

Municipal

Building plan approval from the City of Cape Town or Stellenbosch Municipality, under SANS 10400.

02

Estate or HOA

Design review against the estate's architectural guidelines, usually before council submission.

03

Heritage

Where a building or area is protected, heritage approval may be needed first.

DFees

How fees work

Fees are set out in writing after the first meeting, stage by stage, based on the size and scope of the project. You agree each stage before work on it begins.
